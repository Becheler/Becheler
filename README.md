Freelance C++ engineer based near Paris.

- I currently work on the [Boost Graph Library](https://www.boost.org/doc/libs/latest/libs/graph/doc/html/graph/index.html) for the [C++ Alliance](https://cppalliance.org/), modernizing a 25-year-old library toward C++17 without breaking its users, through deprecation cycles rather than rewrites. In 2026 alone, 170 PRs were merged on `develop`, over 100 of them mine:
    - **Leaner library:** removed around ten Boost dependencies (Spirit, PropertyTree, MPL, Range, Math, Multiprecision, Bimap...), the most common user complaint. C++17 is required from Boost 1.95.
    - **New algorithms:** added Louvain community detection, with more planned.
    - **Docs and tests:** migrated the documentation to AsciiDoc/Antora, rewrote tests that weren't checking anything, and fixed bugs that had been open for years.
    - **Infrastructure:** coverage reporting, sanitizers and strict warnings in CI.
    - **Community:** around 15 new contributors onboarded through good first issues and clear contribution guidelines, plus the [Boost.Graph 2026 Workshop](https://github.com/boostorg/graph/discussions/466) in Paris, which I organized.
- I'm a member of **WG21**, the ISO C++ standards committee, through INCITS (the US national body), sponsored by the C++ Alliance. I take part in review discussions of standards proposals, recently [P3100](https://www.open-std.org/jtc1/sc22/wg21/docs/papers/2025/p3100r5.pdf) and [P4324](https://www.open-std.org/jtc1/sc22/wg21/docs/papers/2026/p4324r0.html).
- I regularly serve as a **Review Manager** for Boost library submissions ([Boost.Bloom](https://www.boost.org/doc/libs/develop/libs/bloom/doc/html/bloom.html), [Boost.Int128](https://develop.int128.cpp.al/overview.html)).
